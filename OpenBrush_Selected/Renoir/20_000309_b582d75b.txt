**SUBJECT:** A young girl with light brown hair styled in a bun, wearing a vibrant red dress with puffed sleeves and a white lace collar.

**ACTION:** Sitting and reading an open book, her head tilted down with focused attention.

**SETTING:** An indoor setting with a dark green background that provides contrast to the subject, creating an intimate and focused atmosphere.

**MOOD:** Quiet, contemplative, and serene.

**STYLE:** Painted in an impressionist style using oil on canvas. The work features characteristic visible brushstrokes that blend softly, creating a sense of movement and texture, particularly in the red dress. The rendering emphasizes light and color over fine detail, with a smooth blending of tones to give the portrait an airy feel.

**LIGHTING:** Soft and diffused, coming from the front-left, creating gentle highlights on her face, hair, and the book.

**COLOR:** Dominated by warm tones of red in the dress against cool green background hues, with subtle variations creating a balanced and harmonious composition.

**COMPOSITION:** A three-quarter perspective from slightly above, capturing the girl in a full figure as she sits. The focus is clearly on the girl and her quiet moment of reading, with the dark background enhancing the sense of depth and intimacy.
